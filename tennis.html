Rules & policies

Payment & scheduling

  • Rates are per student; full payment in advance reserves your place for the season.
  • 10-lesson packages are valid for 100 days, with flexible scheduling to avoid missed lessons.
  • Unused lessons can carry to the next season but aren't deducted from its payment.

Make-ups & cancellations

  • Private lessons: cancel at least 48 hours ahead; late cancellations are non-refundable.
  • Group and semi-private lessons are non-refundable and can't be rescheduled.
  • You may send a replacement player if the coach is told in advance.

Rain policy

  • Rainouts are valid only if the instructor declares them at least an hour ahead.
  • Interrupted within 10 minutes: rescheduled. 10–25 minutes: half charge. Over 25 minutes: full charge.

Groups & registration

  • Group lessons need at least 3 players.
  • Registration closes 4 hours before class time.

The first session is always free.
