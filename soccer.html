Classes for every stage.

Established in 2021, Spain Up Football Academy brings the methodology of the great Spanish clubs to Tokyo. Our coaches are certified by the Spanish Football Federation (RFEF), and every session is enjoyable, age-appropriate and built around possession football.

Rules & policies

Club uniform

Training requires the official club uniform. Damaged kit can be replaced on request; another team's kit isn't allowed in training.

Cancellation

Cancel membership any time. Just email us before the 15th of the previous month.

Make-up classes

Missed a class? Join a make-up class on another day within the same month. Missed classes don't carry over.

The first session is always free.
