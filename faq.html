Ask us anything

Frequently asked questions.

What happens if my child can't attend a class?

You can join a make-up class during the same month. Unused classes cannot be carried over to other months.

If I want to cancel, when should I let you know?

Please tell us by the 15th of the previous month. Payments are processed on the 27th, so later notice means the following month is still charged.

Does my child need experience?

No. Every programme welcomes all ages and levels, and the first session is a free trial.

The first session is always free.
