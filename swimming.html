How a lesson works

Meet & change

We meet at the entrance and head to the changing rooms together.

Warm up

Stretching and foot games before getting into the water.

Swim swim

35 minutes of real practice in the pool.

Wind down

Breathing practice to relax. Then a game to finish group classes with a smile.

Rules & policies

Swim attire

Swimsuit, swim cap and goggles are required.

Cancellations

Private lessons: cancel at least 24 hours ahead or the full fee is charged. Group lessons have no make-ups. We rent the pool with limited places.

Access & viewing

  • No bicycles near the school premises; strollers stay on B1.
  • Parents can watch from the viewing corner.
  • Poolside benches require swim attire, even without entering the water.

The first session is always free.
