The team.

Our coaches and teachers are the foundation of everything. Experienced professionals with strong backgrounds as athletes and educators, many having competed or coached at high levels. On the field, in the pool or in the classroom, they create environments where children thrive.

Head Coach

Degree in Physical Activity and Sports Sciences · UEFA Level 1 football coach · certified personal trainer and nutrition coach · lifeguard and swimming coach · former semi-professional player with over 16 years of coaching.

13-year professional career that began at Club América, facing Manchester City and Juventus in international friendlies and representing Mexico at youth level. FC Barcelona coaching diploma, JFA U-6 Leader.
